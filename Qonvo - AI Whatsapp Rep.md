# Qonvo — Product & Services Definition

## One-line
Qonvo is an AI customer representative that lives on a business's WhatsApp number — it answers customers 24/7 from the business's own knowledge, holds real conversations by text **and voice**, and takes action (books appointments, logs leads, updates sheets/CRM, places orders) on the business's behalf.

## The elevator pitch
Most small and mid-sized businesses lose money in the gap between "customer messages" and "someone replies." Messages arrive at night, on weekends, during rushes, in different languages — and go unanswered, or answered hours late. Qonvo closes that gap. It's a virtual front-desk employee that never sleeps, never forgets an answer, speaks every customer's language, and actually *does the work* — not just a chatbot that deflects, but an agent that books the slot, records the order, and follows up.

---

## What Qonvo actually is

Qonvo is a **managed, per-business AI agent connected to WhatsApp**. Each customer (the business) gets:

1. **A WhatsApp presence** — either their existing WhatsApp Business number connected to Qonvo, or a new number we provision. Customers of *their* business simply message that number like they'd message a human.
2. **A private knowledge brain** — we ingest the business's information (FAQs, price lists, service menus, policies, hours, location, website, documents, product catalog) into a private, per-business knowledge base. Qonvo answers strictly from this — grounded, accurate, no made-up answers.
3. **An action layer (agentic)** — Qonvo doesn't just talk; it's wired to tools. It can read/write a calendar, append to a spreadsheet, create a CRM record, look up an order, trigger a notification, etc. This is what turns it from "chatbot" into "representative."
4. **A voice layer (optional)** — it can listen to a customer's voice note, understand it, and reply with a natural voice note, in the customer's language.
5. **A control panel** — the business owner gets a dashboard to upload/edit knowledge, see conversations, set business rules, and hand off to a human when needed.

It is **fully managed**: the business does not run servers, manage AI models, or write code. They give us their info and their goals; we operate the agent for them.

## The core product (Base offering)

Everything in Qonvo is built on this foundation. The **base service** includes:

- **24/7 automated replies on WhatsApp** to inbound customer messages.
- **Knowledge-grounded answers** — trained on the business's FAQs, pricing, services, hours, policies, location. Answers only from approved knowledge; says "let me connect you to the team" when it doesn't know instead of guessing.
- **Natural, on-brand conversation** — configurable tone/persona (formal, friendly, concise), business name and identity baked in.
- **One primary language** (the business's main market language).
- **Basic lead capture** — name, contact, and intent captured from the chat and stored.
- **One core integration** — e.g. a calendar *or* a Google Sheet, for the single most important action (usually "book an appointment" or "log this inquiry").
- **Human handoff signal** — recognizes when to escalate and notifies the business / pauses the bot so a human can take over.
- **Business hours & fallback rules** — custom auto-messages, off-topic handling, spam filtering.
- **Owner dashboard** — knowledge editing, conversation history, basic settings.
- **Managed onboarding** — we set up the number, ingest the knowledge, configure the persona, and test it before go-live.

> In short: the base tier makes sure **no customer message goes unanswered**, and the single most valuable action (booking / lead capture) happens automatically.

## Value-Added Services (VAS) — modular add-ons

These are sold on top of the base, individually or bundled into higher tiers. This is where the margin and the "possibilities are endless" story live.

### 1. Voice conversations (voicemail in & out)
- **Voice-note understanding** — customer sends a WhatsApp voice note; Qonvo transcribes and understands it.
- **Voice-note replies** — Qonvo answers back with a natural-sounding voice note, not just text.
- **Same-language voice** — replies in the language the customer spoke (Urdu, English, Arabic, Spanish, etc.).
- **Premium voice option** — higher-fidelity, more human, brand-consistent voice persona.
- *Why it matters:* huge for markets and demographics that prefer voice over typing; a major differentiator over text-only bots.

### 2. Multilingual support
- **Auto language detection** — replies in whatever language the customer writes/speaks.
- **Multi-market packs** — support a defined set of languages (e.g. 3, 5, or unlimited) for businesses serving mixed audiences.
- Consistent persona and accuracy across languages.

### 3. Custom agentic workflows
The headline VAS — bespoke "skills" that let Qonvo *do things*, tailored to the business:
- **Appointment booking & rescheduling** — real-time calendar availability, confirmations, reminders, cancellations.
- **Order taking** — build a cart in chat, confirm, and push the order to a sheet/system.
- **Lead qualification** — ask a scripted set of questions, score/segment the lead, route hot leads to sales instantly.
- **Data logging** — write structured records to Google Sheets, Airtable, or a database.
- **CRM sync** — create/update contacts and deals (HubSpot, Zoho, etc.).
- **Lookups** — order status, booking status, account balance, delivery tracking (read from the business's system).
- **Multi-step chained flows** — e.g. *qualify → check availability → book → add to CRM → send reminder*, all in one conversation.
- **Notifications & handoffs** — ping a human on Slack/WhatsApp/email when a condition is met (VIP customer, complaint, high-value lead).
- *These are scoped and built per business; complex/custom workflows are a premium engagement.*

### 4. Integrations
- Calendars: Google Calendar, Outlook, Calendly.
- Sheets/DBs: Google Sheets, Airtable.
- CRMs: HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive.
- E-commerce: Shopify / WooCommerce (order status, catalog).
- Payments **status/links** (surface a payment link or check status — not handling card data directly).
- Custom API integration for businesses with their own systems.

### 5. Proactive / outbound messaging
- **Reminders & follow-ups** — appointment reminders, "you left items in cart," re-engagement.
- **Broadcasts / campaigns** — opt-in promotional or informational blasts to customer lists (within WhatsApp policy).
- **Post-interaction follow-up** — review requests, satisfaction checks.

### 6. Human-in-the-loop & team features
- **Live agent handoff** with a shared inbox.
- **Multiple numbers / departments** (sales vs support).
- **Team seats** for staff to monitor and jump in.

### 7. Analytics & insights
- Conversation volume, response times, resolution rate.
- Top questions & knowledge gaps (what customers ask that the bot couldn't answer → feeds knowledge improvements).
- Leads/bookings generated, conversion tracking.
- Language breakdown, peak-hour analysis.

### 8. Knowledge & content management
- **Managed knowledge updates** — we keep the knowledge base current for the business.
- **Website / document auto-ingestion** — pull and refresh from their site or uploaded docs.
- **Self-service editor** — the business updates answers themselves.

### 9. White-label & enterprise
- **White-label** the dashboard/persona for agencies reselling Qonvo.
- **Dedicated infrastructure / SLA** for high-volume or compliance-sensitive clients.
- **On-brand voice + custom model behavior**.
- **Bespoke build** — fully custom agent for a specific vertical or large client.

## How it works (plain-English flow)
1. A customer messages the business's WhatsApp (text or voice).
2. Qonvo understands the message (transcribes it first if it's voice), detects the language.
3. It looks up the answer in the business's private knowledge base.
4. If the request needs an action, it calls the right tool (check calendar, write to sheet, look up order…).
5. It replies in the customer's language, by text or voice.
6. If it hits something outside its scope or a defined trigger, it hands off to a human and notifies the team.
7. Everything is logged; the owner sees it all in the dashboard.
